FAQ

Good questions.

Is this legal advice?
No. PolicyLens gives informational summaries to help you understand what you’re agreeing to. It isn’t a lawyer and isn’t a substitute for one.
Which browsers does it support?
Any Chromium browser — Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave and similar. It installs as a standard browser extension.
Is my browsing private?
PolicyLens only reads a page when you ask it to analyze one. It looks at the policy and terms text on that page — not your general browsing — and your saved reports stay in your browser.
How is the Privacy Score calculated?
The score is based on factors including data collection, tracking practices, third-party sharing, user controls, retention policies, and other privacy-related risks. Higher scores indicate stronger privacy protections.
